# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Elevator installers and maintenance companies (conservadores) in Argentina, mostly in Buenos Aires. They come looking for a specific spare part, component or complete kit, often with a stopped elevator waiting ("equipo parado"), and need to know whether H. Trimarchi has it and at what price.

## Product Purpose

Website of H. Trimarchi S.R.L., a distributor and manufacturer of elevator components founded in 1981. It must do two things in equal measure: convey that this is the sector's leading company with the longest track record, and make it fast to find a product and request a quote (availability and price).

## Positioning

- Founded in March 1981 by Héctor Trimarchi; family company, 40 years celebrated in 2021.
- More than 9,000 products in stock (national and imported), spare parts for every brand installed in the country.
- Official representative of Fermator (doors), Moris (hydraulic power units and pistons), Montanari Giulio (traction and gearless machines), Monteferro (guide rails) and IPH SAICF (steel wire ropes); also works with Weco, Gervall, Dynatech and SJEC.
- Own manufacturing: cabins made to measure, complete installation kits.
- Two sites in Buenos Aires: head office with warehouses, factory and offices at Abraham J. Luppi 1535 (Villa Pompeya); branch at Tte. Gral. Eustaquio Frías 182 (Parque Centenario).
- 35 employees, many with more than 20 years in the company.

## Operating Context

- Visitors identify parts by brand, model and the equipment's nameplate; a photo of the part or the plate helps.
- Requests are quotes, not online purchases: no prices, stock levels or checkout are published.
- Twelve product lines: spare parts, installation kits, cabins, hydraulic power units, machines, pistons, doors, guide rails, steel wire ropes, control cable and compensation chains, escalators, controllers.
- Technical data lives in tables (rope diameters and breaking loads, conductor sections, chain and tie specifications, escalator specs) and in manufacturer datasheets (Moris and Montanari PDFs).
- 360° product videos exist on the current site for hydraulic units, machines and pistons (MP4 files); not yet connected in the redesign.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML site (Tailwind via CDN, Lucide icons), catalogue data shared in `assets/site.js`; options A (root), B (`opcion-b/`) and C (`opcion-c/`) already exist as alternative proposals.
- No form backend yet: the contact form opens the visitor's mail client with the request filled in.
- Undecided: phone / WhatsApp number, opening hours per site, descriptions of Weco, Gervall, Dynatech and SJEC.
- Google reviews (4.7 / 357 head office, 4.7 / 236 branch) can only be shown live through Google's Places API with a key the company provides; reviews must not be copied by hand.

## Brand Commitments

- The existing logo (H. Trimarchi S.R.L., "Materiales y elementos para el ascensor") and its institutional navy blue must always be kept.
- Formal voice: the visitor is addressed as "usted".

## Evidence on Hand

- Company photographs hosted on trimarchi.com.ar: team at the 1981–2021 anniversary, cabin assembly in the plant, workshop, press brake, warehouses and shelving, product photos and technical drawings for every line.
- Real figures above (1981, +9,000 products, 35 employees, 2 sites) and the brand list.
- No testimonials, client names, case studies or press: none may be invented. Google review texts are not on hand.

## Product Principles

1. Leadership is shown with facts (year, stock, brands, own plant), never with superlatives.
2. Every product page leads to a quote in one step; an urgent request ("equipo parado") is always reachable.
3. Technical information is published as readable text, not images.
4. Real photographs of the company and its products come before any generic imagery.
