# Opción D · la opción C refinada

Scope: `opcion-d/` (inicio, nosotros, productos, producto, marcas, contacto). Mode: Persuade.

This is a refinement, not a new world. The user chose option C as their favourite and asked for it to be optimised with taste-skill, impeccable and Emil Kowalski's motion rules. It inherits C's world and must keep it:
- ivory ground, Cormorant Garamond and Manrope, navy and gold
- symmetry
- the home hero with the plant photo fading diagonally, text aligned right and the badge at the bottom left
- B's tools: search, quote list, filters, urgent request

Refinements applied:
- No labels above headings.
- No thin rules, and no shadows at rest outside overlays.
- No monospace: figures use tabular Manrope.
- Drawn stars instead of glyphs.
- Headlines of two lines at most.
- Real pictures in every page's first screen.
- The 360° videos connected, with poster frames.
- The Google rating shown as real figures; the review cards appear only when they load live.
- Tailwind compiled ahead of time (`assets/d.min.css`), self-hosted fonts, an inline subset of Lucide icons.
- Motion on custom ease-out curves, under 300ms for UI, with press feedback and reduced-motion fallbacks.

Open decisions:
- phone and WhatsApp number
- opening hours
- descriptions of Weco, Gervall, Dynatech and SJEC
- a Google Places API key for live reviews

The earlier "letrero fileteado" option D was discarded by the user on 2026-10-25.
